01 / THE FILM

A new legend
comes to life.

The Legend of Zelda is an upcoming live-action fantasy adventure based on Nintendo's long-running game series. Nintendo, Arad Productions and Sony Pictures are bringing the film to cinemas worldwide in 2027.

The movie is an original story built from the ideas, characters and world developed across four decades of Zelda games. Plot details are still being kept closely guarded.

04 / PRODUCTION

From Hyrule
to the big screen.

2023

Nintendo and Sony Pictures announce development of a live-action Zelda film. Wes Ball is attached to direct.

2025

Benjamin Evan Ainsworth and Bo Bragason are announced as Link and Zelda.

2025–26

Principal photography takes place in New Zealand.

2026

Nintendo officially confirms the title The Legend of Zelda and the April 30, 2027 worldwide theatrical release.
